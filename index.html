Three-Bucket Strategy

Systematic withdrawal approach designed to weather any market cycle

1

Cash Bucket

Years 1–2 · ~10%

Immediate liquidity. No market risk. Replenished from dividends or bond interest. Target: 1–2 years of withdrawals in VMFXX or money market.

2

Income Bucket

Years 3–10 · ~40%

Bonds, dividend stocks (BND, NOBL, HDV, VGSH). Generates income to refill Bucket 1. Provides stability and inflation buffer.

3

Growth Bucket

Years 10+ · ~50%

Long-term equities and REITs (VTI, SCHD, VNQ, QQQ). Grows through full market cycles. Liquidated during strong markets to refill Buckets 1 & 2.

Golden Rules
4%
The Withdrawal Rule

Withdraw no more than 4% per year. Historically sustains a 30-year retirement with high probability across most market environments.

50
50/50 Balance

Half in safe income-producing assets, half in growth. Rebalance annually. Never let growth drift above 60% or below 40%.

2×
Cash Reserve

Always hold 2 years of living expenses in cash or money market. Prevents forced selling during downturns.

95
Plan to Age 95

Build projections to 95 minimum. Longevity is the most underestimated retirement risk. Sequence risk peaks in years 1–10.
